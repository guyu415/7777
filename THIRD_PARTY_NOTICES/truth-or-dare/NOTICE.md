# Truth or Dare game inspiration

The paired truth/dare deck selection and immediate-repeat avoidance in this
application were adapted from ITCraftship's open-source Truth or Dare game.
The original Flutter UI is not bundled. The Chinese couples card content and
the resident-chat integration are original adaptations for this application.
