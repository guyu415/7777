- Every primary pointer press produces a soft expanding blue glow; a held press grows to a bounded radius and fades on release. Keyboard activation also produces a glow; scripted cover-menu replay does not double it. The decorative manual popover stays above native dialogs without consuming input; non-popover browsers use a fixed-layer fallback.
- Holding for 430 ms on non-interactive story surface temporarily uses the existing read-only skip. Release/cancel/drag/background/blur stop it. Choices, menus, unread text, ending cards and ink holds do not auto-advance. Release clicks from a long press are consumed to avoid an extra accidental advance. Text scrolling and sliders retain their normal behavior.
- Reduced-motion settings reduce glow movement and show the ink image fully, while keeping manual dismissal. No idle animation loop runs after a glow finishes.
